Privacy policy

Sedla, last updated 25 October 2026

Sedla keeps your receipts on your iPhone. The developer runs no servers, collects no data and cannot see your receipts, your photos or your email.

What Sedla stores

The receipts you add (photos, PDFs, emails and files), the text Sedla reads from them, and the details you see in the app (store, date, amounts, items). All of it is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. Deleting Sedla deletes it. Sedla has no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

Photos and camera

With your permission, Sedla uses the camera to scan receipts and looks through your photo library to find photos of receipts. Photos are checked on your iPhone. Sedla keeps a copy only of the photos that are receipts.

Gmail

If you connect Gmail, Sedla asks Google for read-only access to your email (the gmail.readonly permission). Sedla uses it only to find receipts and order confirmations in your inbox and to add them to the app. It never sends, changes, labels or deletes email.

  • Your email is downloaded straight from Google to your iPhone and checked there. It is not sent to the developer or anyone else.
  • Only emails that are receipts are kept, as receipts in Sedla. Other emails are not stored.
  • The sign-in token is stored in your iPhone's keychain.
  • You can disconnect Gmail in Sedla's settings at any time, or remove Sedla's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Receipts already added stay in the app until you delete them.

Sedla's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to anyone, not read by humans, and not used to train AI models.

Apple Intelligence

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Sedla uses Apple's on-device model to read receipts and to decide whether a photo or email is a receipt. This runs on your iPhone.

Backups and sharing

Your receipts leave your iPhone only when you choose to: for example when you export a backup, share a receipt, or when your iPhone's own iCloud backup includes the app's data.

Children

Sedla is not aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
