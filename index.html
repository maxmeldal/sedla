Sedla

Your receipts, on your iPhone.

Sedla is an iPhone app that keeps your receipts in one searchable place. Scan a paper receipt, share a PDF, or let Sedla find receipts in your photos and your Gmail. It reads the store, date, total and items so you can find a receipt again when you need it for a return, a warranty or your budget.

Everything happens on your iPhone. Sedla has no servers and no account, and your receipts never leave your phone.
